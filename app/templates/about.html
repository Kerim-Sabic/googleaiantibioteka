About BAA-2025

Bosnia & Herzegovina Antibiotic Advisor

Mission

The BAA-2025 system bridges the gap between international clinical guidelines and the logistical reality of the Bosnia & Herzegovina pharmaceutical market. It provides hospital-grade antibiotic recommendations using ONLY medications registered in the Registar lijekova BiH 2025.

The "Hard-Coded" Philosophy

In clinical settings, hallucination is malpractice. The BAA-2025 operates on a "Closed-World Assumption" - it is forbidden from recommending drugs not listed in the 2025 registry.

If international guidelines recommend Nafcillin for MSSA, the system automatically substitutes it with Kloksacilin (Syntarpen) - the only beta-lactamase resistant penicillin available in BiH.

System Architecture

  • Deterministic Logic: Hard-coded decision trees, not probabilistic AI
  • Registry-Based: Every recommendation verified against BiH 2025 registry
  • Brand-Specific: Outputs exact trade names (e.g., "Longaceph", "Azaran", "Sumamed")
  • Regulatory Compliance: Enforces ZU vs Rp restrictions
